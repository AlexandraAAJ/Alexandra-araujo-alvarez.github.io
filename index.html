Alexandra Araujo Alvarez J

Building high-performing teams and communities at the intersection of people, policy and AI

People are at the heart of everything I do. Whether leading a 250-person cultural organisation in Peru or building research communities in the UK, my focus has always been the same: bringing together talented people, creating the conditions for them to do their best work, and building teams that achieve more together than any of us could alone.

My aspiration is to be a trusted ecosystem connector at the intersection of people, policy and AI, convening researchers, practitioners and decision-makers to shape responsible governance in high-impact contexts. I am motivated by expanding participation in conversations about AI, improving public understanding of its benefits, risks and limitations, and ensuring governance reflects diverse global perspectives. As someone from the Global South, I care deeply about fair and inclusive data and AI governance.

Today I am Senior Research Community Manager for BridgeAI at The Alan Turing Institute, where I connect businesses, AI experts and the wider innovation ecosystem to turn AI into real-world impact. I joined the Institute to manage The Turing Way, a global open-source community, after leading international research programmes at Queen Mary University of London.

Earlier, I spent seven years as Chief Executive of Asociación Cultural Drama, Peru's largest private cultural organisation, and founded an award-winning food brand in London. Alongside my role, I serve as Executive Advisor to the Centre for Doctoral Training in Artificial Intelligence at Coventry University, co-chair The Turing Way Book Dash Working Group, contribute to the RCM Cooperative and mentor women leaders in Latin America through Laboratoria.

Ser Hamlet (documentary)

Co-producer · Teatro La Plaza · Released August 2026

I co-produced Ser Hamlet, a feature documentary directed by Chela De Ferrari that follows a year of rehearsals with eight actors with Down syndrome and intellectual disability. The film had its world premiere at the 30th Lima International Film Festival PUCP in August 2026.

The documentary tells the story behind Teatro La Plaza's acclaimed stage adaptation of Hamlet, which premiered in Lima in 2019 and has since toured more than 50 countries across five continents.

BridgeAI case studies

I led the development and publication of a series of AI deployment case studies, bringing together our technical writer, Independent Scientific Advisors and participating organisations to turn practical experience into accessible stories. The series raises the profile of the organisations involved, celebrates our advisors' expertise and shares lessons with others looking to adopt AI. All are openly available on Zenodo.

  1. Reimagining the wheel: when mechanical engineering meets AI

    How AI can enhance Cyclopic's autonomous electric vehicle platform, including balancing and manoeuvring mobile robots in complex environments.

  2. FarmSmarter: sowing the seeds of an AI agricultural revolution

    AI-powered smartphone tools giving farmers crop-health insights, weather forecasts, field mapping and yield predictions.

  3. Mutus Tech: a vision to transform agriculture through AI

    AI-driven precision-farming tools for crop monitoring, pest management, fertiliser use and sustainability.

  4. OceanOS: AI-driven insights to secure the future of our marine environments

    AI and data tools that monitor marine biodiversity and help offshore-energy operators minimise environmental impact.

  5. Zori Tex: reducing the environmental impact of the fashion industry

    Machine vision, hyperspectral imaging and machine learning to identify textile fibres and improve clothing recycling.

  6. The Independent Scientific Advisor programme

    How expert scientific and technical advice helps SMEs overcome barriers to AI adoption and identify practical opportunities.

  7. Eyya Group Ltd: enhancing train maintenance with AI-powered condition reporting

    AI-powered condition reporting to improve fault identification and inform maintenance decisions.

  8. Low Carbon Impact: using AI insights to reduce transport emissions

    How data and AI help organisations understand transport emissions and reduce their carbon footprint.

  9. Tomale AI: bringing children's stories to life with AI

    Generative AI for personalised, illustrated children's stories, addressing narrative coherence and character consistency.

  10. TrackCarbon: building an environmental impact pipeline for the food industry

    A repeatable data-science pipeline assessing food products' emissions, land use and water consumption.

  11. E-Nano: applying AI and computer vision to real-world turf challenges

    AI and computer vision applied to practical turf-management challenges through automated analysis.

  12. EDGE Digital Manufacturing: making the case for digital investment through data and AI

    How data and AI help manufacturers assess digital investment opportunities and make better-informed decisions.

  13. LinearWorks: enhancing industrial quality control with AI and hyperspectral imaging

    AI and hyperspectral imaging to improve industrial inspection and quality control.

  14. Klimatise: tackling buildings' energy waste with AI-driven comfort control

    AI-driven building controls balancing occupant comfort with energy efficiency.

  15. ViableSite: unlocking the potential of small residential sites through AI automation

    AI automation to assess the viability of small residential development sites and support investment decisions.

  16. Mutus Tech: enhancing pest management tools for Ghanaian farmers with BridgeAI internship support

    AI-supported pest management, developed with internship support, helping Ghanaian farmers identify crop pests and access advice.

  17. costify.ai: transforming construction cost data into AI-powered insights

    AI that turns construction cost data into actionable insights for estimating, analysis and decision-making.

  18. Useful Simple Trust: exploring practical AI applications across an employee-owned consultancy

    Practical opportunities for AI adoption within an employee-owned consultancy and its services.

  19. SparkSoft: building an AI toolkit to help farmers detect and manage crop pests

    AI tools for crop-pest detection and management, developed with BridgeAI internship support.
